Toll-free verification

Twilio toll-free verification rejection codes and their fixes

Every code below carries Twilio’s own name and the start of its fix, quoted, grouped the way Twilio groups them, with a link to Twilio’s full text. 127 come from Twilio’s help article Why Was My Toll-Free Verification Rejected? (read 20 November 2026); 30817 comes from its own error page.

Got a rejection? Paste it into the free fixer: you get Twilio’s fix for each code, the time you have left to edit, and a check of your privacy policy and SMS terms. Nothing you paste leaves your browser.

How long you have to edit

Twilio: …gives you seven days to amend a rejected submission while retaining its review priority.

After the seven days it loses that priority, but you can still send corrections for a new review.

Why Was My Toll-Free Verification Rejected?, read 20 November 2026

30493, 30536 and 30817: Twilio’s pages disagree

  1. Submissions missing these links will be rejected with error 30493. (Twilio's Toll-Free Verification Onboarding Guide, read 20 Nov 2026)
  2. Correct missing, inaccessible, or incomplete Terms of Service. Privacy Policy issues are separate under 30536. (Twilio's help article 'Why Was My Toll-Free Verification Rejected?' (updated guidance for 30493), read 20 Nov 2026)
  3. …rejected because the Privacy Policy URL, the Terms and Conditions URL, or both were missing… (Twilio's error page for 30817, updated 1 May 2026)

Twilio's pages disagree. Go by the reason text Twilio sent with your code.
